# Hakiland Display second kerning pass

Ready for integration:223 exact kerning changes, no glyph or metric changes.

Inspected732 unique ordered pairs from `(ATVYFL × E) ∪ (E × ATVYFL)`, where E is52Latin letters plus12punctuation characters.36 baseline OFF/ON sheets at50px×3 and12final H/n context sheets at32px×3 were opened and reviewed.223 changes received7baseline/trial pair-comparison sheets. All28word/control rows were inspected at40px×3 and24px×1 using identical black ink in both rows.

The largest omitted patterns were Td/Tq and short stems under T, missing V/Y lowercase neighbors, reverse mixed-case bowls and short forms before T/V/Y, F lower cavities, and punctuation/quotes beside overhangs or receding diagonals. Caps with facing top serifs and tall ascenders stayed restrained. The original We improvement and H/O/n/o rhythm are unchanged.

The root and design peer independently reviewed changed sheets; the ligature peer reviewed final contexts513–732. Root independently reviewed all display/native word sheets. No unresolved concerns within this declared matrix;509pairs retained their existing values.

This is a bounded pass, not whole-font pair coverage. Figures, other symbols, straight quotes, hyphens, unsupported accents/scripts and pairs with neither side in ATVYFL were excluded. Synthetic context covers rare/mixed-case strings for which natural words do not exist. Ligature assessment and the four f+ascenderpositive values belong to the separate ligature report.

Use proposed-kerning.json for explicit before/after values and reasons; coverage.json for exact case-sensitive scope, observations, source hashes, feature settings and evidence. Baseline and trial TTF snapshots and all SVGs/PNGs remain in this directory.
